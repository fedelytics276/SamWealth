## 🎓 Key Lessons Embedded

### **1. Core-Out, Not Product-Out**
Build infrastructure first (Security Master, Data Plane, Accounting Engine), then products. This is how Bloomberg, FactSet, and Morningstar scaled.

### **2. Compliance as Data, Not Code**
Store regulatory rules in Neo4j graph, not hardcoded in application logic. This makes regulatory adaptation trivial.

### **3. Evidence Trails are Product**
Don't hide the reasoning — make it the main feature. Users trust what they understand.

### **4. African-First, Not Africa-Only**
Build for African markets first (underserved, high growth), but design for global expansion (standards-compliant from Day 1).

### **5. LTV:CAC is King**
Unit economics (18x LTV:CAC) matter more than top-line growth. Profitable customers > vanity metrics.

## ⚠️ Critical Success Factors

### **1. Hire Head of Engineering Week 1**
This is your most important hire. Get this wrong, and everything else fails.

**Target Profile**:
- 10+ years engineering
- 5+ years leadership
- Startup experience (0→1 products)
- Graph database knowledge (or strong learning ability)
- African market awareness (bonus)

**Where to Find**:
- LinkedIn (ex-FAANG engineers interested in impact)
- African tech hubs (Lagos, Nairobi, Cape Town)
- Referrals from network

---

### **2. Launch with JSE Only (Don't Boil the Ocean)**
Start with 1 market (JSE — highest quality data), not 7 markets simultaneously.

**Why**:
- Data quality > data quantity
- Easier to debug issues
- Faster to product-market fit

**Expand Later**: Add NGX (Month 6), KE/GH/EG (Month 9), TZ/UG (Month 12)

---

### **3. Focus on ERAU (North Star Metric)**
Not all users are equal. Focus on users who:
1. View evidence trails (engaged)
2. Act on recommendations (trusting)

**ERAU = Evidence trails viewed × Acceptance rate**

Target: 30% acceptance rate (industry benchmark: 10%)

---

### **4. Data Quality is Non-Negotiable**
Bad data → bad recommendations → user churn → platform failure.

Invest in:
- Robust ingestion pipelines
- Data quality checks (5+ validation rules)
- Manual spot-checking (weekly)
- User feedback loops ("Was this data accurate?")

---

### **5. Regulatory Engagement from Day 1**
Don't wait until you're big to talk to regulators. Engage early:
- FSCA sandbox (South Africa): Apply Month 3
- SEC Nigeria fintech license: Apply Month 6
- CMA Kenya authorization: Apply Month 9

**Why**: Regulatory approval takes 6-12 months. Start early.
