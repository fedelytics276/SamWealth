## 1. Hiring Strategy Overview

### 1.1 Hiring Principles

**Quality over Speed**:
- Hire slowly, fire fast
- A-players hire A-players
- Bar raiser in every interview loop
- Better to miss a quarter than hire wrong person

**Remote-First**:
- Hire globally, not just locally
- Timezone overlap: 4 hours with African hours
- Async communication culture
- Annual offsites for team bonding

**Diversity as Strength**:
- 50% African team (local market knowledge)
- 50% international (global best practices)
- Gender diversity target: 40% women
- Experience diversity: mix of big tech + startup veterans

**Mission-Driven**:
- Hire people who care about African markets
- Not just mercenaries chasing equity
- Cultural fit matters as much as skills

## 3. Job Descriptions

### 3.1 Head of Engineering

**Role**: Lead technical strategy and team

**Responsibilities**:
- Own technical architecture and roadmap
- Build and manage engineering team (8-12 people Phase 1)
- Ensure code quality, security, scalability
- Interface with CEO on product-tech tradeoffs
- Establish engineering culture and processes

**Requirements**:
- 10+ years software engineering
- 5+ years engineering leadership
- Experience with distributed systems, databases at scale
- Built 0→1 products (startup experience essential)
- Bonus: Graph database expertise, African market knowledge

**Nice to Have**:
- CTO experience at Series A+ startup
- Fintech background
- Open source contributions

**Compensation**:
- Salary: $180K-$220K
- Equity: 2-4%
- Location: Remote (must overlap 4+ hours with African timezone)

**Interview Process**: 4 rounds
1. CEO screen (30 min): Culture fit, motivation
2. Technical deep dive (90 min): Architecture design, system design
3. Leadership case study (60 min): "How would you build engineering team?"
4. Team fit (45 min × 2): Meet founders + early engineers

## 4. Interview Process

### 4.1 Standard Process Flow

**Stage 1: Application** (0-3 days)
- Candidate applies via website or referral
- Recruiter reviews resume + cover letter
- Decision: Reject or move to screen

**Stage 2: Recruiter Screen** (30 min)
- Verify experience, motivation
- Explain role, company, compensation range
- Answer candidate questions
- Decision: Reject or move to technical

**Stage 3: Technical Interview** (60-90 min)
- Role-specific assessment (see job descriptions)
- Technical depth + problem-solving
- Decision: Reject, maybe, or strong yes

**Stage 4: Team Fit** (30-45 min × 2)
- Meet potential teammates
- Cultural fit assessment
- Values alignment
- Decision: Hire or pass

**Stage 5: Offer** (same day as Stage 4)
- Verbal offer immediately after positive Team Fit
- Written offer within 24 hours
- 1-week deadline to accept

**Total Timeline**: 2 weeks from application to offer

---

### 4.2 Interview Questions Bank

**For Engineers**:
- "Tell me about the most complex system you've built. What would you do differently?"
- "How do you approach code review? What do you look for?"
- "Describe a time you had to make a technical tradeoff. How did you decide?"
- Live coding: Relevant to role (see job descriptions)

**For Product**:
- "How do you prioritize features when everything is important?"
- "Walk me through a product you shipped that failed. What did you learn?"
- "How do you validate user needs before building?"
- Case study: Feature prioritization exercise

**For Everyone** (Cultural Fit):
- "Why MOBU? Why now?"
- "Describe your ideal work environment"
- "Tell me about a time you disagreed with your manager. How did you handle it?"
- "What's something you believe that most people disagree with?"

**Red Flags** (Auto-Reject):
- Bad-mouthing previous employer
- Can't articulate why they want to join
- Arrogant or dismissive
- Not curious (no questions for us)

|-------|-------------|--------------|--------------|

- Annual refreshers for top performers (0.1-0.2% additional equity)

---

### 5.2 Equity Philosophy

**Total Employee Option Pool**: 15% (post-seed)

**Distribution**:
- Head of Engineering: 2-4%
- Head of Product: 1-2%
- First 5 engineers: 0.5-1% each
- Next 10 employees: 0.2-0.6% each
- Future employees (Phase 1B+): Pool remainder

**Vesting Schedule**:
- 4 years total
- 1-year cliff (25% vests after 1 year)
- Quarterly vesting thereafter (1/16 per quarter)

**Early Exercise**:
- Allowed for employees in low-tax jurisdictions
- Helps with tax optimization

## 7. Sourcing Strategies

### 7.1 Sourcing Channels

**1. Network Referrals** (30% of hires — best quality):
- Founder networks
- Investor introductions
- Employee referrals (bonus: $2K for engineer, $1K for others)

**2. LinkedIn Outreach** (25% of hires):
- Recruiter searches (Boolean queries)
- Direct outreach to passive candidates
- Target: Ex-FAANG, fintech, African tech hubs

**3. Job Boards** (20% of hires):
- AngelList/Wellfound (startup-focused)
- Hacker News "Who's Hiring"
- African boards: Jobberman, BrighterMonday
- Remote boards: We Work Remotely, RemoteOK

**4. Universities** (15% of hires — junior roles):
- University of Cape Town, University of Witwatersrand (SA)
- Carnegie Mellon Africa, AIMS (science/math)
- Campus recruiting (internship → full-time pipeline)

**5. Agencies** (10% of hires — hard-to-fill roles):
- Specialized tech recruiters
- African talent agencies
- Contingency fee: 20% of first-year salary

## 9. Retention Strategy

### 9.1 Keeping Top Talent

**Career Growth**:
- Quarterly performance reviews
- Clear IC (Individual Contributor) and management tracks
- Learning budgets ($1K/year)
- Conference attendance (1-2/year)

**Mission Connection**:
- Monthly all-hands (vision, progress, wins)
- User testimonials shared
- Impact stories ("Thabo used MOBU to buy his first home")

**Equity Refreshers**:
- Top 20% performers: Annual equity grants (0.1-0.2%)
- Promotes: Equity bump (0.2-0.5%)
- Retention offers if approached by competitors

**Work-Life Balance**:
- No hero culture (40-hour weeks standard)
- Unlimited PTO (encouraged to take 25+ days)
- Remote-first, flexible hours

---

### 9.2 Red Flags for Attrition

**Watch For**:
- Decreased code contributions
- Missing meetings
- Negative Slack tone
- LinkedIn profile updates
- Asking about vesting schedule

**Intervention**:
- Manager 1:1 (monthly → weekly)
- Address issues (boredom, conflict, compensation)
- Retention offer if flight risk

**Exit Process**:
- If leaving, exit interview
- Knowledge transfer (2-week notice minimum)
- Alumni network (stay in touch)

## Conclusion

Hiring is the most important thing founders do in Phase 1. Get the first 15 people right, and the rest follows. Get them wrong, and no amount of capital fixes it.

**Key Success Factors**:
1. **Hire slowly**: Better to wait for A-player than settle
2. **Sell the mission**: African transparency matters
3. **Competitive comp**: Match big tech where it counts
4. **Remote-first**: Access global talent pool
5. **Diversity**: African + international, junior + senior

**Target State (Month 6)**:
- 15 people
- <10% regrettable attrition
- 100% would refer a friend (eNPS)
- Shipping features weekly
