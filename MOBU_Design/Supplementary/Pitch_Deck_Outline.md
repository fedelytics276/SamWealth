## Presentation Tips

### **Opening (First 2 minutes are critical)**
- Start with the problem: "95% of African investors don't know why stocks are recommended to them"
- Hook: "After 2020 platform failures, trust collapsed. Regulators are asking: 'Can you explain your recommendations?' Most can't. We can."

### **Middle (Build the case)**
- Problem → Solution → Market → Product → Traction → Team
- Use visuals, not text walls
- Tell a story: "Meet Thabo, 35, Johannesburg, has R500K to invest..."

### **Close (Leave them wanting more)**
- Vision: "We're not just building for Africa—we're setting the global standard"
- Ask: "Join us in bringing transparent investment intelligence to 150M African investors"

### **Q&A Preparation**
Have ready answers for:
1. "What if Bloomberg enters African markets?"
2. "How do you defend against copycats?"
3. "Why now? Why you?"
4. "What's your regulatory risk?"
5. "How do you acquire users cheaply?"
6. "What's your take rate?"
7. "Why is this hard?"
8. "What keeps you up at night?"

## Design Guidelines

### **Visual Style**
- Clean, professional (think Sequoia pitch deck style)
- MOBU brand colors (blue, white, subtle grays)
- Charts > Text
- High-quality graphics (no clipart)
- Consistent font (Inter or Helvetica)

### **Data Visualization**
- Use real data from financial models
- Show trends, not just numbers
- Annotate key inflection points
- Color code: green = good, red = risk, blue = neutral

### **Imagery**
- Screenshots of actual product
- Map of Africa with markets highlighted
- Team photos (professional headshots)
- Evidence trail visualization (your unique differentiator)

## Delivery Formats

**PowerPoint/Keynote**:
- Standard pitch format
- 16:9 aspect ratio
- Include animations (subtle)
- PDF backup

**Video Deck** (for async):
- Record 15-minute walkthrough
- Narrate each slide
- Upload to private Vimeo
- Include in email to investors

**One-Pager**:
- Condensed version (1 page)
- Problem, solution, traction, ask
- Use as leave-behind

## Success Metrics

**You've nailed it if investors say**:
- "I get it—this is the transparent alternative"
- "The graph architecture is a real moat"
- "Your unit economics are compelling"
- "I see the African opportunity clearly"
- "When can I wire the money?"

**Red flags if they say**:
- "Interesting, but isn't this just better UX?"
- "I don't understand the technical differentiation"
- "Africa feels too risky for me"
- "How is this different from robo-advisors?"

If you hear red flags → refine messaging, add clarity
